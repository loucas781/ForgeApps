Privacy Policy

Applies to PayForge, ForgeShift, ForgeTrack, WatchCue, Fruit Squash, and this website.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Who publishes these services

The apps and website covered here are independently published by Louis Birch. Forge Apps is the product collection name for PayForge, ForgeShift, and ForgeTrack.

Scope

This policy covers the software and website published by independent creator Louis Birch: the Forge Apps collection (PayForge, ForgeShift, and ForgeTrack), WatchCue, Fruit Squash, and the support website. WatchCue and Fruit Squash are separate from Forge Apps but are part of the wider portfolio.

App data

Our applications are designed to minimise data collection. App data is primarily stored on your device unless a feature requires a connected service, you enable a synchronisation feature, or you explicitly choose to share information.

WatchCue

WatchCue stores the viewing information you choose to create, such as your library, episode and season progress, title status, ratings, film notes, settings, and reminder preferences. Saved library data can be kept available through local caching. If you choose to use WatchCue's iCloud backup and restore features, the relevant backup data is sent to and stored through Apple's iCloud services under your Apple account and Apple's privacy policies. WatchCue also supports exporting and importing your viewing data at your request.

WatchCue may request notification permission so it can deliver release reminders for titles you follow. You control whether notifications are allowed in your device settings. We do not receive the content of your personal library, ratings, or notes merely because you use the app.

Fruit Squash

Fruit Squash is a mobile game available through the App Store and Google Play. The App Store privacy disclosure for Fruit Squash lists location information, identifiers, and usage data (including advertising data) as data that may be collected by advertising, analytics, and product-personalisation services; it also indicates that location information may be used for tracking. Android data practices are described in the Google Play Data safety section. Store disclosures and the services used can differ by platform and may change over time.

Support messages

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include in your email, such as your name, email address, device details, screenshots, and description of the issue. We use that information only to respond to and investigate your request.

Purchases

For purchases made through an app store, the relevant store provider processes billing and payment data according to its policies. Apple processes App Store purchases under Apple's policies, and Google processes Google Play purchases under Google's policies. We do not receive your full payment details.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as applications and their features evolve. The date above indicates when the policy was most recently revised.
